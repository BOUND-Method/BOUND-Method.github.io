RESEARCH

Evidence, experiments, and reproducibility.

The research area should document what BOUND claims, how those claims are evaluated, and where the evidence remains incomplete.

Publications

Use the existing BOUND v3.0 publication and IFEM material as the canonical historical record. The website should link to archival sources rather than duplicate long manuscripts.

Research questions

  • Does explicit responsibility ownership reduce cross-boundary ambiguity?
  • Do formalized contracts reduce integration defects?
  • Does bounded execution reduce coordination overhead in parallel work?
  • How do BOUND artifacts affect AI-assisted development?

Potential measurements

  • Integration defects
  • Contract violations
  • Coordination events
  • Time to integration
  • Rollback frequency
  • Agent scope violations
  • Verification failures

Evidence policy

Do not claim adoption, validation, rankings, awards, endorsements, or industry success without documented evidence. When benchmarks exist, publish the methodology, dataset, raw results, analysis, and limitations.

Research program

Research should separate proposed questions from executed experiments and observed evidence. See benchmark design for candidate measurements and reproducibility guidance for the required research record.
